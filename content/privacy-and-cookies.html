Privacy and Cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to measure traffic. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, device, browser and approximate location. You can block these cookies in your browser settings. Pages do not ask you to create an account or enter personal data. If you email editors@best-auto-parts-websites.com, we receive your address and your message, and we use them only to answer you and to correct the site. Links to store and government websites open sites with their own privacy and cookie practices, which this notice does not cover. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs, such as the time of a visit, the page address and the browser type. We do not use these logs to identify individual readers. Email messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them and to document a correction. To ask us to delete your message, write to the same address and name the date you wrote. This notice may change, and the page date shows the last update. The site does not sell or share your email address with stores or advertisers.
