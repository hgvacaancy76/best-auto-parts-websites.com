Import taxes in Australia

The Australian Taxation Office says GST applies at the point of sale in the same way as for Australian stores, and that it generally does not apply where an overseas supplier is below the registration threshold. Customs duty and clearance charges may apply at the border. For goods with a customs value above A$1,000, GST is payable at the border. The tax office page was last updated on 11 September 2025, so confirm that nothing has changed since then.

Before you order

  1. Enter an Australian address at checkout to confirm the store ships there.

  2. Check whether GST is charged at checkout, and avoid paying it twice.

  3. Remember that shipping and insurance count toward the price for GST.

  4. Read return terms before paying, because return shipping can be costly.

  5. Keep the invoice and the carrier's tracking number for customs.

  6. Ask the carrier whether duty is collected on delivery.

If a store does not ship to Australia

If a store does not ship to Australia, a forwarder can receive the parcel at a United States address and send it on. Global Shopaholics and MyUS both list Australia as a destination. Forwarders add fees and customs steps. Read the forwarders guide before choosing one, and compare the full quote.

Frequently asked questions

Which stores ship car parts to Australia?

RockAuto says it ships everywhere in the world unless prohibited by law, and Cars245 states worldwide shipping. FCP Euro offers international shipping, so confirm Australia at checkout. Autodoc does not list Australia. Check the table for each store's status.

Do I pay GST on car parts from overseas?

The tax office says GST applies at the point of sale in the same way as for local stores, and it may apply at the border for goods above A$1,000. If you paid GST at the point of sale, the Border Force cannot refund GST paid at the border.

Will I pay customs duty as well?

The tax office says you may also need to pay customs duty and clearance charges applicable at the border. The amount depends on the part and its value. Ask the store or carrier which tariff code is on the invoice before you order.
