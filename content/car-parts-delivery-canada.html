Import taxes in Canada

The Canada Border Services Agency says an item worth CAN$20 or less sent by mail carries no duty or tax. Above CAN$20 you pay the applicable duty, GST or HST and any PST on the item's full value. The page lists exceptions, so read it before ordering. Duty rates depend on the type of goods. Stores may collect some taxes at checkout, and the carrier may collect the rest at delivery.

Before you order

  1. Enter a Canadian postal code at checkout to see the store's quote.

  2. Check whether GST, HST or duties are charged at checkout or on delivery.

  3. Read return terms, because the buyer pays return shipping at several stores.

  4. Confirm fitment by OEM number and VIN for your vehicle.

  5. Keep the commercial invoice, which RockAuto sends when the warehouse and the delivery address are in different countries.

  6. Remember that RockAuto says duties cannot be refunded on a return.

If a store does not ship to Canada

If a store does not ship to Canada, a forwarder can receive the parcel at a United States address and send it on. Global Shopaholics publishes a guide for buying from RockAuto and shipping to Canada. Forwarders add fees and customs steps. Read the forwarders guide first, and compare the total.

Frequently asked questions

Does RockAuto ship to Canada?

RockAuto's help page says shipping is available everywhere in the world unless prohibited by law, and a quote appears after you enter your country and postal code in the cart. It also says it collects GST or HST, with duties and brokerage fees, when the order is placed.

Which ranked stores do not ship to Canada?

CarParts.com states that it ships only within the continental United States. O'Reilly Auto Parts states that it does not ship outside the 50 United States and Puerto Rico. NAPA's online shipping page lists US states only, although NAPA runs a separate Canadian site.

Is there a duty-free limit for parts sent to Canada?

The CBSA says items worth CAN$20 or less sent by mail carry no duty or tax. Above that, duty, GST or HST and any PST apply to the full value. Check the CBSA page for exceptions before ordering.
