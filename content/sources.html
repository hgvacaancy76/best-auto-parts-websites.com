Authority sources

  • U.S. Federal Register, rule 2026-12669: the $800 de minimis exemption stays suspended for mail shipments to the United States, with a new postal informal entry process.

  • GOV.UK, tax and duty on goods sent from abroad: VAT and Customs Duty for goods sent to Great Britain.

  • Canada Border Services Agency, courier and mail imports: duty and tax rules for items sent by mail or courier.

  • Australian Taxation Office, consumers importing goods: GST at sale and at the border, and customs duty on goods bought from overseas suppliers.

  • Government of Israel: official entry point for the customs and tax authority pages that set personal import thresholds in Israel.

  • Certified Automotive Parts Association: the certification program for aftermarket crash parts such as fenders, hoods and lighting, used on the OEM guide.
