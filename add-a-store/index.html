Suggest a Store for the Ranking

Who can submit

Anyone can suggest a store: a buyer, a seller or a reader who has used it. The store must sell new OEM or aftermarket parts online to private buyers and publish readable shipping, return and payment pages. Used parts, tuning catalogs and wholesale-only catalogs are outside the scope of this ranking. A store that ships to a limited area can still be suggested, since the ranking shows delivery limits.

What to send

  • Send the store's name and website address.
  • Add links to its shipping, returns and payment pages, the countries it ships to, and its customer rating page if one exists.
  • If you represent the store, say so in the message.
  • Please do not send screenshots of private orders or other personal data.
  • Every figure we publish must link to a public page, so a message without links may take longer to process.
  • Tell us which country you ordered from or would ship to, because delivery rules differ by market.

What happens next

The editorial team reads the pages you sent and checks them against the selection rules on the ranking methodology page. We answer when the check is finished. A suggestion does not guarantee a listing, and a listing does not guarantee a position. A store cannot pay for a place, and its position follows the published criteria.

Submit a store

Send your suggestion by email to editors@best-auto-parts-websites.com with the store name and links.
