Import taxes in Israel

Israel changed its personal import thresholds in December 2025, and a DHL notice says the threshold fell from US$150 to US$130 on 25 February 2026. The same notice says shipments above US$500 face import duties and purchase tax. Sources differ on the current figures, so read the official pages on gov.il before ordering parts. A part can be held at customs until the tax is paid, so ask the carrier how payment works. Thresholds apply to the value of the whole shipment, not to a single item, according to carrier notices.

Before you order

  1. Enter an Israeli address at checkout to confirm the store ships there.

  2. Ask the store or carrier which duties and taxes apply to your part.

  3. Expect that purchase tax can apply to some goods, and check it on gov.il.

  4. Match the part by OEM number and VIN before paying.

  5. Keep the invoice and the tracking number for customs, and ask whether a forwarder or the store handles the paperwork.

If a store does not ship to Israel

If a store does not ship to Israel, a forwarder can receive the parcel at a United States address and send it on. MyUS names Israel among its example destinations. Forwarders add fees and customs steps, and the same Israeli rules still apply. Read the forwarders guide first. Compare the total.

Frequently asked questions

Which stores ship car parts to Israel?

RockAuto says it ships everywhere in the world unless prohibited by law, and Cars245 states worldwide shipping. FCP Euro offers international shipping, so confirm Israel at checkout. Autodoc does not list Israel. Check the table above for each store's status.

What is the Israeli duty-free limit for online orders?

Sources differ. Carriers described changes in December 2025 and in February 2026, including a DHL notice of US$130 for VAT from 25 February 2026. We do not print a single figure as current. Read the official pages on gov.il before ordering.

Does purchase tax apply to car parts?

Purchase tax can apply to some goods, and a DHL notice says shipments above US$500 face import duties and purchase tax. Whether it applies to your part depends on the part and its value. Confirm with the carrier or the Israeli authorities before paying.
