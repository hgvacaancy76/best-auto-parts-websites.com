Editorial Policy: Sourcing, Corrections and Store Requests

By Editorial team/Editorial policy/Last updated: November 2026

Principles

The site is written by an editorial team and published without personal bylines. Pages describe stores in the same structure and with the same criteria, and a store receives no special wording because of its position. Statements that a store makes about itself are labeled as store claims. The site does not describe tests, purchases or expert reviews unless the page links to the source. Each page shows the date of its last check. The ranking method is described on the ranking methodology page.

Sourcing rules

Every number needs a source link and a check date. Preferred sources are the store's own policy pages, government customs and tax pages, and the public profile of the rating platform. Secondary sources, such as forwarder guides or news reports, are used only for context and are named as such. User comments and forum posts are not stated as facts about a store. If a source cannot be opened on the check date, the figure is not published. A missing figure is better than a figure that cannot be checked.

Conflicting data

When two sources disagree, the page prefers the primary source and shows its date. If the disagreement cannot be settled, the page says that sources differ and links to the official page instead of printing a single number. This happens most often with import thresholds, which several countries have changed within a year. Readers should confirm the current rule on the government page before ordering.

Corrections

Readers can report an error by email with the page address, the figure and a source link. The editorial team reads the source, corrects the page when the report is confirmed and updates the date shown under the headline. Reports that cannot be checked against a public source are logged and not applied. Corrections are listed in the update log below.

Store requests

A store can ask for a review of its listing by sending the page address and a link to the policy page that supports the change. Requests do not change a store's position by themselves. Data is updated when the source confirms it. To propose a new store, use the add a store page.

Update log

18 November 2026: first edition published. Store data, ratings and import rules for five markets were collected and dated. No corrections have been made yet.
