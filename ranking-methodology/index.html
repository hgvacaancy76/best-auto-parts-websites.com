Ranking Methodology: How Online Auto Parts Stores Are Compared

By Editorial team/Editorial policy/Last updated: November 2026

Purpose and scope

This page explains how the ranking of online auto parts stores is built. The ranking covers stores that sell new OEM and aftermarket parts online to private buyers. It does not cover used parts, tuning catalogs, wholesale-only catalogs or website design. Its purpose is to help a buyer compare stores on the same published criteria and then verify each figure at the source.

Selection rules

A store enters the ranking when it sells new parts online, accepts orders from private buyers and has a public website with readable shipping, return and payment pages. The list also includes the chains that readers and search results mention most often for this topic, so that buyers can compare them with smaller specialists. Stores that sell mainly tires with in-store pickup, or that do not publish their terms, are left out until their pages can be read. A store can be added later if its pages meet these rules.

Criteria definitions

Delivery countries

The countries a store states that it ships to.

Delivery time

The handling or transit time the store publishes. Estimates are marked as store claims, because the actual time depends on the carrier, the address and the day the order is placed.

Delivery cost

The shipping rules the store publishes, such as free shipping thresholds. The ranking does not compare part prices, because they change daily.

Catalog size

The number of parts the store states on its own pages. Figures from the store are marked as store claims and are not counted by this site.

Part number search

Whether the store lets buyers search by OEM or manufacturer part number. Where the pages do not say, the field stays empty, and the check does not assume that a large store offers it.

OEM and aftermarket

Whether the store says it sells original equipment parts, aftermarket parts or both, using the wording on its own pages, since a store may call its parts stock replacement or branded.

Returns

The return window, who pays return shipping and which conditions apply, such as new, unused parts in original packaging. Refund rules for shipping and duties are noted when published.

Payment

The payment methods the store lists on its help or checkout pages, such as cards, PayPal or bank transfer.

Import duties

How the store handles duties and taxes for parcels sent abroad: collected at order, left to the carrier on delivery, or not stated.

Data sources and dates

Every number in the tables and cards links to a source page and shows the date it was checked. Primary sources are the store's own shipping, returns and help pages, government customs pages and the rating platform's public profile. Data in this edition was collected on 18 November 2026, except where a table shows another date. The check date is shown next to the figure, and older figures should be rechecked.

How positions are set

Positions are an editorial judgment, not a weighted score. The judgment asks how well a store serves a buyer who needs new OEM or aftermarket parts shipped outside the store's home market, using the nine criteria above. The site publishes no composite score and no formula, so readers should compare the criteria that matter to them. Cars245 holds the first position because it states worldwide shipping from an EU warehouse and OEM number search, and its limits are shown in its card. A buyer in the United States who needs a part the same day may reasonably prefer a chain with pickup counters.

Reputation data

Customer ratings come from one platform, Trustpilot, for all ten stores, with the score, the number of reviews and the date of the check. Ratings describe what reviewers on that platform reported. They do not describe every customer's experience, and they can change daily. The rating date appears next to each score.

Updates and corrections

The data is rechecked when a store changes its terms or when a reader reports an error. The date of the last check appears on each page. To report a mistake, write to editors@best-auto-parts-websites.com with the page, the figure and a source link. Corrections are made after the source is read, and the page date is updated.

Limits

The ranking has limits. Store pages change without notice, and shipping or duty rules differ by address, parcel and carrier. Some stores do not publish every criterion. Import duty rules change, so the figures shown are dated and linked to government pages. This site gives information, not legal, tax or customs advice, so confirm the final total at checkout and read the store's current terms.
