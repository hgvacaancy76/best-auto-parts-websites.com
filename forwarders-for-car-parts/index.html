Package Forwarders for Car Parts

By Editorial team/Editorial policy/Last updated: November 2026

A package forwarder gives you an address in the store's country, receives your parcel there and ships it to you. It can help when a store ships only within the United States. It adds fees, customs steps and sometimes slower returns, and some stores restrict forwarders. Compare the forwarder's quote with a store that ships to your country directly, and check the store's rules first.

How package forwarding works

  1. 1

    You sign up with a forwarder and receive a street address in the United States or another store country.

  2. 2

    You enter that address at the store's checkout.

  3. 3

    The store ships to the forwarder, which receives and stores the parcel, then ships it onward to you with a carrier you choose.

  4. 4

    Import duties and taxes in your country still apply.

Some forwarders consolidate several parcels into one box. Check their storage time, fees and weight limits before the first order, because charges differ by service.

Will the store accept a forwarder address

Not every store accepts forwarder addresses. PartsGeek, according to a forwarder guide, only ships to US addresses and may decline cards with non-US billing addresses. Several forwarder guides note that stores can block forwarders altogether. Read the store's shipping and payment pages before you ship a heavy part to a forwarder.

Package forwarders compared

MyUS

MyUS gives members a US address and a UK address. Premium members get 30 days of free package storage, and Israel, Canada and Australia appear among its example destinations.

ForwardMe

ForwardMe offers a shipping calculator with price quotes for 220 countries, according to its blog on RockAuto orders from the United States.

Stackry

Stackry provides a free US address, 45 days of free storage after packages arrive, and says it ships worldwide, with the full country list on its site.

Planet Express

Planet Express says it can forward car parts to almost any country, with ship-from locations in the United States and United Kingdom. Parts containing liquids cannot be shipped.

Reship

Reship publishes a guide to forwarding RockAuto orders to buyers abroad. Confirm its country coverage, fees and storage rules on its site before you ship a part.

Global Shopaholics

Global Shopaholics says it serves more than 200 countries and territories, and publishes guides for buying from RockAuto and PartsGeek and shipping to Canada or Australia.

Easy Delivery

Easy Delivery publishes a page about delivery from Autodoc to Israel. Check its quote, delivery time and customs handling on its site before relying on it for a heavy part.

Risks to check

Forwarders add fees for receiving, storing and shipping, and they do not remove import duties. Warranty and return handling can be harder, because RockAuto says it cannot provide a return label from its site when the ship-to country differs from the bill-to country. Cards with non-US billing addresses may be declined at some stores. Heavy or liquid-filled parts can be refused or cost more to ship. Compare the full quote before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use a forwarder for car parts?

Use one when the store you want ships only within its home country and no store that ships directly to you offers the part. If a ranked store already delivers to your country, compare its total price with the forwarder's quote first.

Does a forwarder pay my import duties?

No. A forwarder moves the parcel, and customs duties and taxes in your country still apply. Some forwarders offer help with customs paperwork or prepayment, so read their terms. Compare the duty rules for your market on the import duties page.

Can I return a part through a forwarder?

Returns are harder. Return shipping to the store can be costly, and a store may not provide a label when the ship-to country differs from the bill-to country. Check the store's return policy and the forwarder's return service before you buy an expensive part.
